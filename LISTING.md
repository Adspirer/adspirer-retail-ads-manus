## Short description

Amazon & Walmart retail advertising. Analyze ad performance, track ACoS and ROAS, research keywords, and create and optimize campaigns with your approval.

## Long description

Adspirer Retail Ads connects Manus to your advertising accounts through a hosted MCP server. It covers
Amazon Ads and Walmart Connect. It can change ad accounts, and only with your approval: campaigns are built paused, every change is shown first and read back afterwards. The agent never purchases or upgrades a subscription on your behalf. Sign-in
is browser OAuth with your Adspirer account; ad accounts are connected at
https://adspirer.ai/connections. An Adspirer account and an applicable plan are required.

## Example prompts

- "Show ACoS and ROAS by campaign for my Amazon Sponsored Products last month."
- "Find negative keyword candidates in my Amazon search terms."
- "Which Walmart items are out of budget or underperforming this week?"

## Getting listed

Manus's documentation describes no submission process for third-party connectors. Users add
Adspirer Retail Ads as a custom MCP server with the URL above, and upload the skills. If Manus opens a
connector directory or you have a partner contact, the details on this page are what they will
ask for.
